My first trip to the United States, and I did the journey properly: Bengaluru to Las Vegas in **business class**, with a layover in **Paris** on the way. Lie-flat seats, a window over the Alps, and a few hours in Paris to break up the haul — the flying was half the holiday. I've never arrived anywhere feeling that fresh.

## The city that runs on neon

Then Vegas. There's really nothing like it. A whole city of glass towers and giant screens rising straight out of the desert, glowing all night like someone left the lights on for the entire planet. By day you can see the dry mountains ringing the valley; by night the Strip turns into pure spectacle. I walked past Treasure Island lit up in gold and just grinned — it's so much, and that's the point.

## The Sphere

The highlight was the **Sphere** — the enormous wraparound venue — for a **Backstreet Boys** concert. I don't have the words for what that room does to a show. The sound wraps completely around you, the visuals fill your whole field of view, and the whole crowd is singing along to songs we all grew up on. Easily one of the best live experiences of my life.

## Notes for next time

- If you can swing business class on a long haul, do it once. It changes the whole trip.
- A layover in a city you love (Paris, for me) turns dead travel time into a bonus stop.
- Catch something at the Sphere. Any show. Just go.

> Deserts, neon, and a boy band from my childhood — Vegas had no business being this much fun, and it absolutely was.
